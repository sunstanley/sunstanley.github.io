Offcut Step Stool

An improvised little step stool made entirely from offcuts I picked out of the garbage bin — sugar maple bark edge trimmings and a narrow strip of walnut.

  1. Cut the walnut in half and glue it up to make a wider board.
  2. Shape the maple trimmings into legs with a spokeshave.
  3. Cut the tops of the legs into tapered round tenons so that continued use tightens the joints over time.
  4. Keep it simple: a single nail pins each leg in place.
